## Ongoing cadence (Phase 3 onward)

- 1 post every 3 to 4 weeks after the initial 6, sustainable pace beats a burst followed by silence
- Rotate topics across all 3 channels (D2C, food, export) so no single audience segment is neglected
- Every post must cite a real fact from the actual product catalog, real FAQ content, or a cited external source (FSSAI, BIS packaging standards, etc), never invented statistics

## What NOT to do

- Do not publish thin, templated per city blog posts ("Packaging Manufacturer in Madurai", "Packaging Manufacturer in Coimbatore" etc) purely to target city keywords. This is the blog equivalent of the doorway page pattern already rejected for landing pages, and Google's helpful content systems specifically target this pattern.
- Do not use AI generated content without a human review and fact check pass, especially on regulatory claims (FSSAI, export compliance). Wrong claims here are a business risk, not just an SEO one.
